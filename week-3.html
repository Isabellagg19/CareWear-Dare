✨ Your mission this week ✨

Take some time to research local brands, thrift stores, or designers in your city that focus on ethical or sustainable clothing. You might be surprised by how many creative alternatives already exist.

Choose at least one brand or store that inspires you and learn a little about what makes their fashion more sustainable.

Share what you discover with a friend or on social media, remember

inspiring others is part of the change
