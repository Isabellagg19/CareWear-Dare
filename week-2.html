Upcycling 101

Upcycling is about turning something you no longer use into something better. It means transforming clothes or materials that have lost their purpose into pieces with higher creative, ecological, and even economic value. Instead of throwing things away, you redesign them. You give them a second life with intention.

✂️ Crop it

Turn long shirts into cropped tops.

🎨 Dye it

Give faded clothes a new color.

🧵 Patch it

Cover stains with embroidery or fabric patches.

👖 Reshape it

Turn jeans into shorts.

🎀 Restyle it

Add buttons, cut sleeves, layer differently.
